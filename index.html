Forward Projection & Sensitivity

Of the five dimensions, two are forward-looking under uncertainty and are simulated to 2030: D2 (economic viability) via a Monte Carlo LSMC model of battery arbitrage under stochastic prices, and D4 (demand growth) under demand scenarios. The remaining three (D1, D3, D5) are assessed on current observed state and are shown below as fixed point estimates — they are not projected.

The Revenue stacking toggle adds an illustrative second bar showing how battery viability changes once non-arbitrage revenue streams (frequency response, balancing, capacity) are included alongside arbitrage — most visibly for the United Kingdom, where arbitrage alone does not clear but the full stack does. This overlay is a 2025-2026 market snapshot for visual context only; revenue-stacking depth is already captured in the D3 (Storage Market Maturity) score and this toggle does not change any composite or dimension score.
